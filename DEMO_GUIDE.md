# Demonstration guide

1. Start the app as described in README. The seed creates four fictional crew members and synthetic 14-day histories.
2. In Dashboard, rotate and zoom the astronaut suit model. Its floating animation plays automatically.
3. Select another demo profile in the top bar. Health Monitoring shows stored observations and period filters.
4. Submit a Mental Health check-in; return to view its saved history and timeline event. Try the breathing timer.
5. Open Reports, switch periods, and download a calculated PDF.
6. In Settings, choose Admin to demonstrate adding/editing/removing fictional crew and manually entering demo measurements. This selector is not secure authorization.
7. Ask the AI assistant a question. Without `GEMINI_API_KEY`, it truthfully reports that the service is unavailable.
8. Open Scientific Evidence for official NASA resource links. Medical Protocols has no content until reviewed sources and instructions are provided.
